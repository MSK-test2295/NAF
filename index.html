Questions

Answers before you ask

The things people want to know before getting a rate.

Will checking my rate hurt my credit score?
No. Getting your personalized rate uses a soft credit check, which has no impact on your score. A hard check only happens later, with your permission, if you move forward with a full application.
How much do I need for a down payment?
It depends on the program. Conventional loans can start as low as 3% down for qualified buyers, FHA loans at 3.5%, and VA loans may require nothing down. A specialist can help you find the lowest option you qualify for.
How fast can I actually close?
Most Meridian loans close in around three weeks once your application is complete. Timing depends on the property, appraisal, and how quickly documents come together — and we'll keep you posted at every step.
What documents will I need?
Typically recent pay stubs, W-2s or tax returns, bank statements, and a photo ID. You can upload everything securely online, and your specialist will tell you exactly what's needed for your situation.
Is refinancing worth it for me?
It can be, if a lower rate or shorter term saves you more than the cost of refinancing. We'll show you a clear break-even point up front so you can decide with real numbers, not guesswork.
